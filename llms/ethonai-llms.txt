# EthonAI

> EthonAI AG is an ETH Zurich spin-off (founded 2021, Zurich, Switzerland) that builds an Industrial AI Platform — the Manufacturing Analytics System (MAS). It applies Causal AI through a no-code platform so manufacturing teams can move from reactive to prescriptive quality and process analytics. The platform is API-first (data in/out via MQTT, REST, and a Python SDK), cloud-agnostic (AWS, GCP, Azure), and used by manufacturers including Siemens, Bosch, Roche, and Lindt & Sprüngli. Part of the Siemens Xcelerator ecosystem.
